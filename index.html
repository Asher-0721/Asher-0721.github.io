CROWDFUNDING MEDICAL

Subsidizing the cost of medical expenses

ABOUT

Medical treatment can be costly, and there are certain expenses associated with medical treatment that may not be covered by public healthcare schemes or medical aid. These costs include consultations, medication, transport, and other expenses related to the treatment process. This can place a financial strain on patients with chronic illnesses such as cancer, Alzheimer's disease, dementia, and Parkinson's disease.

Donation crowdfunding is a process that involves raising money from multiple donors through a crowdfunding campaign. The campaign explains the person's needs and appeals to the public, friends, businesses, and community groups for financial support. In South Africa, one of the popular platforms where fundraising campaigns can be created is BackaBuddy.

The aim of this research paper is to examine how donation crowdfunding can assist people with their medical needs in South Africa while ensuring that transparency, ethical fundraising, and ubuntu are maintained. Ubuntu is important because it focuses on helping others and understanding the value of community. However, kindness should also come with responsibility.

DEMENTIA AND AGEING

The Phase 1 planning document used information from Alzheimer's South Africa (ADASA), which estimated that about 187,000 people in South Africa were living with dementia, with the number expected to rise to around 250,000 by 2030.

HOW DONATION CROWDFUNDING WORKS

The medical crowdfunding process usually starts when the patient, a family member, or an authorised representative provides information about the medical requirements and the amount of money needed. The campaign should explain how the funds will be spent and include relevant supporting documentation.

The Phase 1 research was carried out using BackaBuddy’s medical crowdfunding guide. Medical crowdfunding campaigns can share the patient’s story, provide updates on fundraising progress, accept donations, and share news. Promoting the campaign may help raise awareness and attract more donations; however, the success of the campaign cannot be guaranteed through these efforts alone.
